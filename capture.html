Drag on the video to move the ring centre; pinch to resize it. The ring must sit on the path the marker travels. Or let the wheel turn a quarter revolution and tap Auto-fit.

Tap the marker on the video to sample its colour. Widen the hue range only if the lock keeps dropping as the light changes.

If the connection or the server goes away, capture continues at the offline rate into storage on this phone, then replays automatically when it returns. Offline frames are saved small — enough to track the tape, not to watch — so about 13 hours of outage fits at 0.5 fps before the oldest are dropped. A slower rate buys more time; the wheel turns far too slowly for it to cost any accuracy.

Keep the phone plugged in. The screen is held awake while streaming, but iOS still pauses capture if you switch apps or lock the screen — leave this page in the foreground.
